Simple YMD is a small homescreen widget that shows a label and a compact
years / months / days counter for both countdowns and countups.

Example:

Childs birthday
1Y 3M 18D

Features:
* Count up from a past date, or down to a future date
* Per-widget label, date, dark/light colours, background opacity
* 2×1 default size, resizable
* No network, no ads, no tracking
* All data stays on the device

Add the widget from the home screen: long-press → Widgets → Simple YMD.
Tap a widget to edit it.

For a reliable midnight update, allow exact alarms for this app.
On some phones, also set Battery → Unrestricted.
